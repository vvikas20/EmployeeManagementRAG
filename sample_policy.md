# Global Corporate Employee & Workplace Policy (2026)

## 1. Overview, Governance & Applicability
This Global Corporate Employee & Workplace Policy document defines the employment guidelines, statutory and discretionary leave entitlements, performance appreciation protocols, health benefits, and workplace standards for all full-time regular employees, probationers, and contractual staff of the organization.

- **Policy Administrator:** Corporate Human Resources & Operations Department.
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 (supersedes all previous iterations).
- **Scope:** Applicable across all global business entities, delivery centers, corporate offices, and approved remote work arrangements.
- **Equal Employment Opportunity:** The company enforces a zero-tolerance policy against discrimination based on race, gender, religion, age, sexual orientation, disability, or marital status.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Annual Leave Entitlements

The organization provides generous annual paid time-off across categorized leave types to foster work-life equilibrium, personal recuperation, and family welfare:

| Leave Type Code | Leave Category | Annual Allocation | Carry Forward Max | Application Notice & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EL** | **Earned / Privilege Leave** | **30 Days** | Up to 15 days into next calendar year | Credited quarterly (7.5 days on Jan 1, Apr 1, Jul 1, Oct 1). Requires manager approval at least 7 days in advance. |
| **CL** | **Casual Leave** | **12 Days** | Non-cumulative (Lapses on Dec 31) | For urgent personal matters. Max 3 consecutive days at a time. Requires minimum 24-hour prior intimation. |
| **SL** | **Sick Leave** | **12 Days** | Non-cumulative (Lapses on Dec 31) | Intended for illness and medical recovery. Medical certificate required from a licensed physician if absent for more than 2 consecutive days. |
| **ML** | **Maternity Leave** | **26 Weeks (182 Days)** | Fully paid statutory leave | Applicable for female employees with at least 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months. Covers up to 2 surviving children. |
| **PL** | **Paternity Leave** | **15 Working Days** | Must be utilized within 6 months | Fully paid leave for fathers upon childbirth or legal adoption. Can be taken in maximum 2 tranches. |
| **BL** | **Bereavement Leave** | **5 Days** | Per instance (Non-cumulative) | Granted on the demise of immediate family members (parents, spouse, children, siblings, in-laws). |
| **TL** | **Training & Upskilling Leave** | **5 Days** | Non-cumulative | Dedicated paid leave for attending corporate-approved technical bootcamps, certifications, or leadership workshops. |

## 3. Leave Calculation, Probation & Carry-Forward Rules

1. **Total Available Balance Calculation:**
   $$\text{Total Available Annual Leaves} = \text{Remaining Casual Leave (CL)} + \text{Remaining Earned Leave (EL)} + \text{Remaining Sick Leave (SL)}$$
2. **Probation Period Entitlement:**
   - Employees in their initial probationary period (first 6 months from joining) are eligible for **1 Casual Leave per completed month of service**.
   - Earned Leaves accrue during probation but may only be availed after successful confirmation of employment.
3. **Year-End Carry Forward & Encashment:**
   - A maximum of **15 Earned Leave days** will automatically carry forward to the subsequent calendar year. Any unused EL balance above 15 days is subject to encashment at base salary rate upon December payroll cycle.
   - Casual Leave and Sick Leave balances lapse entirely on December 31 each year and cannot be encashed or carried forward.
4. **Leave Without Pay (LWP):**
   - Absences exceeding accumulated leave balances are marked as Leave Without Pay (LWP), subject to written endorsement from the Department Head and HR Business Partner.

## 4. Leave Application Workflow & Manager SLA

- **Self-Service Submission:** All leave requests must be logged through the internal Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal or authorized Multi-Agent Chatbot.
- **Approval SLA:** Reporting Managers must take action (Approve/Reject/Request Reschedule) within **48 business hours** of submission. If unattended after 48 hours, the request automatically escalates to the Departmental Director.
- **Emergency Unplanned Absences:** For unforeseen emergencies or sudden illness, the employee or their representative must notify the reporting manager via email or messaging before 10:00 AM on the day of absence.

## 5. Working Hours, Core Collaboration & Hybrid Work Policy

1. **Standard Working Hours:**
   - Monday to Friday: **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM** (including a 1-hour lunch and wellness break).
2. **Core Collaboration Hours:**
   - **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** are mandatory core collaboration hours across all teams. All employees must be reachable via corporate Slack/Teams and email during these hours regardless of physical location.
3. **Hybrid Workplace Guidelines:**
   - Eligible teams operate on a **3 Days Office / 2 Days Work-From-Home (WFH)** schedule per week.
   - Remote work days must be aligned with the team's weekly sprint planning and mutually agreed upon with the direct line manager.
   - High-speed internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps) and a secure workspace are mandatory prerequisites for remote days.

## 6. Mandatory Corporate Training & Notification Protocol

1. **Mandatory Upskilling Modules:**
   - All employees are required to complete annual Information Security, POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment), Code of Conduct, and Data Privacy compliance modules within 30 days of release.
2. **Line Manager in CC Requirement:**
   - When nominating, inviting, or confirming an employee for internal workshops, cross-departmental training sessions, or external technical upskilling, official communication **MUST explicitly keep the direct Line Manager in CC**.
3. **Certification Reimbursement:**
   - The company sponsors industry-recognized technical and management certifications (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP, PMP, CISSP, Scrum Alliance) up to **$1,500 (or ₹1,25,000)** per fiscal year upon successful exam completion and manager sign-off.

## 7. Performance Recognition, Awards & Certificate Collection

1. **Recognition Programs:**
   - **Quarterly Spotlight Awards:** Recognizes outstanding individual contribution to business deliverables, engineering excellence, or customer advocacy.
   - **Annual Pioneer Awards:** Conferred at the global annual town hall for groundbreaking innovation and leadership.
   - **Long-Service Milestones:** Awarded at 3, 5, 10, and 15 years of continuous service.
2. **Appreciation Certificate Collection Protocol:**
   - Employees awarded formal recognition or appreciation certificates must collect their physical, signed commendation certificates and awards from the **HQ Office Reception Desk / Security Concierge Desk** on the Ground Floor during regular operating hours (10:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday–Friday).
   - An official email notification is dispatched to the employee with their direct Line Manager in CC confirming that their physical certificate is ready for pickup at the reception desk.

## 8. Employee Health, Medical Insurance & Wellness

1. **Group Medical Insurance (GMC):**
   - All full-time employees and their immediate dependants (spouse and up to 2 dependent children) are covered under a comprehensive cashless group health insurance policy with a baseline sum insured of **$10,000 (or ₹10,00,000)** annually.
   - Optional parental coverage can be opted into during the annual open enrollment window in April.
2. **Annual Executive Health Checkup:**
   - Employees aged 30 and above are entitled to an annual comprehensive health screening at empanelled diagnostic centers at 100% company cost.
3. **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):**
   - Free, 100% confidential 24/7 mental wellness and counseling assistance available for employees and their immediate families via the tele-health partner portal.

---

## 9. Business Travel, Per Diem & Expense Reimbursement

1. **Domestic & International Travel Tiers:**
   - Economy class air travel is standard for domestic and short-haul international flights under 6 hours.
   - Business class is permitted for intercontinental flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.
2. **Per Diem Allowances:**
   - Tier-1 Metro Cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad): **$40 (₹3,000) per day** for incidental food and local travel.
   - Tier-2 Cities: **$30 (₹2,200) per day**.
   - International Travel (US/Europe/UK): **$90 per day**.
3. **Reimbursement Submission Window:**
   - All travel expense reports accompanied by valid tax invoices must be submitted via the finance portal within **15 calendar days** of returning from travel.

## 10. Code of Conduct, Grievance Redressal & Absconding Policy

1. **Code of Conduct:**
   - Employees must uphold absolute integrity, confidentiality of proprietary intellectual property, and professional decorum at all times.
2. **Grievance Redressal & Whistleblower Mechanism:**
   - An independent Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles all workplace grievances and POSH complaints with guaranteed anonymity and strict non-retaliation protections.
3. **Unauthorized Absence & Absconding Rule:**
   - Unexplained, unapproved absence for **3 consecutive working days** without any intimation to the reporting manager or HR will be classified as voluntary abandonment of employment (Absconding).
   - In such events, formal show-cause notices will be dispatched to the employee's registered postal and email address prior to initiation of administrative separation.
